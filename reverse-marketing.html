🪃 Reverse marketing: find the response, copy the format, attach it to the core message

Reverse marketing starts from what viewers already answer. We find the video that drew the most responses in a set window, copy its format (never its footage), and use that format to carry our own message through the flywheel.

🔁 The 3 steps

Step 1

🔎 Find the video with the most responses

Rank our videos by comments in a fixed window. The window here is the last 90 days, from 20 Aug 2026.

Step 2

🧩 Reverse-engineer it

Write down the title pattern, length, hook, structure and call to action. Copy the format, never the footage or thumbnail.

Step 3

🧭 Attach it to the core message and flywheel, then make it better

Map the response to the four core ideas and to a flywheel step. Then cut the time and cost of the next one.

🧭 Step 3 · Attach it to the core message and flywheel

Every response names which of the four core ideas it carries, as the core message page requires. Cards with none get cut.

The four ideas
  • 1 · Expectations are rising. The response carries this idea: the "work is ending" claim is about expectations, and our answer gives the data.
  • 2 · From driver to pilot. Our counter-claim is that the job moves to the pilot, who sets direction and checks the result. This is the ✍️ assumption to test in the video.
The flywheel

From business.html:

  1. Students learn for free ← this response video is the entry point
  2. The best get certified as Delivery Pilots
  3. Certified pilots build and run bots
  4. Businesses pay for the managed service
  5. Revenue funds free education for more students

📌 The response video's job is step 1 only. Its end card and description point to the Skool step (step 2), not to a sale.
