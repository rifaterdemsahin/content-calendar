Three audiences, one funnel

Free Shorts reach consumers who want entertainment. Some of them are professionals who move into the Skool community. Some work inside enterprises and move to the website, where they buy managed services. Every piece of content is made for one primary audience and points to the next step.

How we make content for each audience

  1. One primary audience per piece. Shorts are for consumers. Wednesday course videos are for businesses. Clinics and modules are for professionals.
  2. Every piece has a next step. Shorts point to a long video or the Skool community. Long videos point to Skool. Skool posts point to managed services when the work is company-scale.
  3. Hook for consumers, proof for professionals, outcome for businesses. The same topic gets a different framing for each audience.
  4. Track the handoffs. Each CTA has its own link so we can see which audience moves where.
