📌 Reference

🎬 The CTA short, start to finish

A 47-second vertical short cut from one green-screen take. It opens on the hook, bridges with the SaaS line, and ends on the Delivery Pilot call to action with the Skool community at the end.

If the player is blank, open it on Google Drive.

🧭 What the short is made of

🎣 Hook · 8.6 s

"What if you could reverse engineer any video editing style… without spending hours of setting up video production tools."

🌉 Bridge · 7.7 s

"Today, we are building a SaaS tool for the community…" (added to connect the hook to the call to action).

🚀 Call to action · 31 s

The sample business needs a Delivery Pilot. Templates, bots, training and community. Join at deliverypilot.net and the Skool community.

🪜 How we did it

  1. Get the take

    The take is NINJAV_S001_S001_T020.MOV on the NINJAV drive: 1920×1080, about 2 min 55 s. The speech is on audio channels 3 and 4, not the front pair, so a normal stereo player plays it almost silent. We check channels with scripts/video/inspect_audio.sh and move the speech channels to left and right with scripts/video/stereo_playback.sh.

  2. Transcribe

    DeepInfra Whisper large-v3 gives segment timings (about $0.001 for the take). We pick the sentences for each window from those timings and add a 0.15 s pad before and 0.2–0.3 s after.

  3. Key the green screen

    Chroma key at 0x6ec832 (similarity 0.12, blend 0.04) with a green despill. The key only covers the screen area, so the paper, lamps and walls keep their colour. RVM was not run; it needs PyTorch and model weights, which didn't fit on the disk.

  4. Background and music

    The background is a generated studio gradient from FLUX-2-pro (about $0.015). The music bed is a 35 s instrumental from DeepInfra ACE-Step (about $0.035), ducked under the voice.

  5. Captions and graphics

    Captions are drawn with Pillow, one frame per highlight step: the current word is yellow. The graphics (sample business, four provisions, Delivery Pilot badge, learn/fix/adapt, Skool end card) come from the timed spec scripts/video/graphics_cta.json. This ffmpeg build has no subtitle or text filters, so Pillow draws them.

  6. Render

    scripts/video/greenscreen_short.sh keys the clip, crops tight on the speaker (640 px square, filling the bottom 1080 px), places the background, overlays the graphics and captions, mixes the voice (+10 dB, compressed) with the ducked music, and encodes H.264 CRF 18 at 1080×1920 with AAC.

  7. Join the windows

    scripts/video/join_clips.sh joins the three windows with hard video cuts and an 80 ms audio crossfade. We tried a dissolve first, but it left a ghosted double image of the speaker.

  8. Edit in Hyperframes

    The finished short is the base layer of a portrait Hyperframes project, cta-short, shown in Studio on your Mac at localhost:3002. Studio runs only on that machine, so it isn't on this page. The project's skill and CLI updates are a step for the owner.

  9. Store and share

    The file went to the Synology share GoogleDrivePexaboShared, which syncs to Google Drive, then was shared as "anyone with the link can view". Keys were read from Azure Key Vault (dp-kv-deliverypilot) and never written to files or to this page.

⚠️ Still to check

  • Whisper heard "the Pro-I templates" where the speaker said "We provide the templates". The captions use the corrected words. Confirm against the audio.
  • Whisper heard "school community". The captions say "Skool community". Confirm.
  • The voice is still quiet on average (about -28 dBFS). Raise it or lower the music if it's hard to hear.
  • The speaker's mouth and hands at the crop edges are upscaled from 1080p, so they are a little soft. A 4K take would fix this.
  • No RVM matte. The edges come from chroma key only.
