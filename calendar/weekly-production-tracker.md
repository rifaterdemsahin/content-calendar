- `[ ] IDEA` — Topic selected and angle confirmed
- `[ ] SCRIPT` — Bullet outline or script completed (using template)

- `[ ] EDIT` — Cuts, B-roll, captions, audio normalized
- `[ ] ASSET` — Skool companion resource ready (JSON, code, PDF)
- `[ ] SCHED` — Video and thumbnail uploaded & scheduled
