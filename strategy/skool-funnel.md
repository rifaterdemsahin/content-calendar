# Skool Community Conversion & Engagement Funnel

How to transform casual YouTube viewers into active, engaged Skool members.

---

## 1. Funnel Architecture

```
YouTube Shorts (30-60s) ───┐
                           ├─► YouTube Long / Course Video ───► Skool Community Landing Page
Direct Shorts Pinned Link ─┘   (Lead magnet / workflow JSON)          │
                                                                       ▼
                                                          Free / Paid Onboarding
                                                                       │
                                      ┌────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┐
                                      ▼                                                               ▼
                             Classroom Courses                                            Community Engagement
                      (Templates, Code, Video Modules)                                (Daily Q&A, Showcases, Events)
```

---

## 2. Lead Magnet Mapping (By Video Type)

| Content Type | What the Viewer Gets on YouTube | What Requires Entering Skool |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shorts** | Quick 45s demonstration or concept | Complete blueprint, prompt cheat sheet, or full-length breakdown. |
| **Long-Form (Tutorials)** | Complete step-by-step video build | Downloadable workflow JSON, Dockerfiles, prompt templates, and discussion thread. |
| **Course Videos** | Full video lecture & visual walkthrough | Slide deck, code repository, homework grading/feedback, live office hours access. |

## 4. Skool Engagement & Retention Playbook

1. **Auto-Welcome Automation:**
   - When a member joins, prompt them to make an introduction post: *"Who are you, what stack do you use, and what is the #1 AI workflow you want to build?"*
2. **Weekly Live Office Hours:**
   - Host a 45-minute weekly Zoom/Live stream for Skool members to review code and debug workflows.
3. **Gamified Levels & Unlocks:**
   - **Level 1:** Access to basic templates & community feed.
   - **Level 2 (Requires 5 likes/comments):** Unlocks the "Delivery Pilot Advanced Agent Playbook".
   - **Level 3:** Unlocks private prompt library & architecture templates.
