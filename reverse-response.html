The idea

Ride the videos that are already growing

Instead of waiting for an audience to find us, we pick a fast-growing YouTube video on a topic we know, make our own video that responds to it, and say so in the rationale. The growing video brings the viewers; our video gives them the next step: the tutorial, the workflow JSON, or Skool.

🔁 The method: three steps

  1. 1 · Find high-response content

    Find fast-growing videos that are similar to ours, from the channel and from outside it. Log each in data/rmm-targets.json.

  2. 2 · Reverse-engineer the format

    Note its length, the first 3-second hook, the on-screen structure and the call to action. Copy the format, never the footage or thumbnail.

  3. 3 · Mix with the core message

    Use the same format to deliver our message (see the core message page). Name the target in the rationale and the description.
