Dashboard 🔁 Reverse Response Nov 2026
The idea

Ride the videos that are already growing

Instead of waiting for an audience to find us, we pick a fast-growing YouTube video on a topic we know, make our own video that responds to it, and say so in the rationale. The growing video brings the viewers; our video gives them the next step: the tutorial, the workflow JSON, or Skool.

The loop

  1. 1 · Spot

    A video in our topics is growing fast on its own channel. Log it in data/rmm-targets.json.

  2. 2 · Respond

    Make a sprint video that answers it: the step it skipped, the fix, or the build it promised.

  3. 3 · Cite

    Name the target in the rationale and the first line of the description. Never copy its footage or thumbnail.

  4. 4 · Hand off

    Pin the Skool link and the workflow file. Erdem learns the topic and shares what he learned.
