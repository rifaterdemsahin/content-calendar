## Video Outline Blueprint

### 1. The Hook (0:00 – 1:30)
- **Show the Outcome First:** Demonstrate the finished working agent or automated result immediately.
- **The Pain Point:** Why traditional approaches take days or break constantly.
- **The Promise:** *"In the next 15 minutes, you will see exactly how to build this yourself step by step."*
- **Community Mention:** *"You don't need to type this from scratch—all templates and code are in our Skool community."*

### 2. Architecture & Mental Model (1:30 – 4:00)
- Simple whiteboard / diagram explanation (Mermaid / Excalidraw).
- How the components connect: Triggers -> Brain (LLM) -> Tools -> Memory -> Actions.
- Establishing the "Delivery Pilot" mindset: You aren't writing lines of code; you are orchestrating system components.

### 3. Step-by-Step Build (4:00 – 16:00)
- Segment 1: Setting up the environment & credentials.
- Segment 2: Prompt engineering & tool definition.
- Segment 3: Handling real-world errors and retry loops.
- Segment 4: Testing with live edge cases.

### 4. Verification & Production Readiness (16:00 – 20:00)
- How to ensure reliability and cost efficiency.
- Security and credential management (e.g., Key Vault / Environment variables).

### 5. Conclusion & Skool Community CTA (20:00 – End)
- Recap key learning points.
- Strong Call to Action:
  > *"If you want the exact workflow file, join our Skool community below. We also have weekly live build sessions where we troubleshoot workflows together. Click the link in the description, grab the template, and let's build."*
