⭐ Early Stars in the AI Niche

A small channel with a video that got many times more views than the channel has subscribers means the topic pulled people in, not the channel. Those are the videos worth making your own version of.

🧮 The rule: views ÷ subscribers
Example: Taha El Harti's editing-style video had 22,125 views on a channel with 4.05k subs on 22 Nov 2026 → 5.5×. ⭐ = 5× or more. 🌱 = 3× to 5×.
⚠️ Judgement, not a law: the 5× line, the 3× floor and the 50k-subs cap are my starting assumptions. Change them in data/early-stars.json. Numbers are a snapshot from the "checked" date on each card. Subscriber counts are rounded by YouTube.
